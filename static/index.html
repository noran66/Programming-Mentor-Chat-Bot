About CodeBuddy

What data it uses

Your code, your test results, your hint history and your ratings in this session. Only fictional or synthetic data should be entered. Nothing is sent to an AI model in this demo build.

What it cannot do

  • Run C++ or Java in this demo build (Python only).
  • Verify complexity: it gives an estimate and shows how it got it.
  • Give the full solution without asking you first.

How to read its answers

Every answer has a confidence label shown in words: Confirmed (backed by a test or a rule), Possible issue, or Not sure. Open Why this? to see what it looked at, why, and what it is unsure about. You can say I disagree at any time.
